Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying numbers, receiving SMS codes, and deposits.

How long does it take to receive an SMS?

SMS messages are received in real-time. Most codes arrive within 5 to 30 seconds after you request it from the respective app.

What happens if I don't receive an SMS code?

You will NOT be charged! If no SMS arrives within the 15-minute countdown, or if you click "Cancel Order", the full cost is automatically and immediately refunded to your balance.

How do I deposit funds via Crypto (USDT, BTC, ETH, SOL, TON...) or Bank Transfer?

Go to the Top Up / Payment page, select any of our 10+ supported Cryptocurrencies (USDT TRC-20, BEP-20, BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, TON, LTC, DOGE, or Binance Pay ID) or Direct Bank Transfer. Copy the deposit address, send your transaction, and submit your TxID/Hash for instant credit.

Can I use these numbers for WhatsApp and Telegram?

Yes! We provide fresh, non-recycled virtual numbers from UK, USA, Canada, Indonesia, Pakistan, and 150+ countries that work smoothly for WhatsApp, Telegram, Google, and all major platforms.
